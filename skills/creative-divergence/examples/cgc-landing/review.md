# Review — the passes

Rendered with `node tools/screen-render.mjs index.html --mobile` at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844
after every change; gated with `node tools/slop-lint.mjs index.html` (clean, 0 of 23, from the
first pass — which proves only that it is not the template). The loop ran until a pass could
name nothing a passionate professional would still change.

## Pass 1

**Desktop.** The hero does what the directions promised: the light italic at display size, the
drawn strike, the insertion in the one colour. Weakest thing: **composition** — the 8/4 split
began at the ledger, so the lede hung alone under the thesis with the right third of the page
empty until the first phase; and the insertion mark sat under the *b* of "nobody" rather than in
the clear space above the struck word.
**Phone.** The correction was illegible: "gate" at ten pixels. The date orphaned onto a second
line of the running head.

**Changes.** The lede moved *into* the wide column, so the split starts directly under the
thesis and the first margin note hangs beside it (the extrapolation: the report's top margin
shrank, the lede gained a bottom margin instead). The insertion moved to 41% of the struck word
and up 0.08 em, into the gap between the *o* and the *d* above. On the phone the thesis grew to
three lines at 15.5 vw, the insertion to 0.3 em (eighteen pixels), and the date left the head.

## Pass 2

**Desktop.** The split now begins under the thesis; the notes align with the lede; the mark sits
cleanly. Weakest thing: **the bold move is timid** — the thesis stops at two-thirds of the
measure. Boldness is spent in one place, and this is the place; it should fill the width.
**Phone.** Legible, three lines, the mark in the clear space above "preference". Nothing to name.

**Changes.** Thesis to 10.9 vw (cap 12 rem), leading 0.9, tracking −0.035 em; the top margin
reduced because the larger type carries its own presence.

## Pass 3

**Desktop.** The sentence runs to the right edge of the measure; the insertion sits in the gap
above the struck word; lede, notes and ledger share one left edge and one top. **Phone.** As
pass 2.

The professional's questions, each a yes: the glance lands on the correction and learns the
thesis; the sentence — *an audit report typeset as a printed ledger, with the thesis copy-edited
in red* — describes no template; with another tool's name in it the rows and the correction stop
making sense; the structure is a ledger grammar and a drawn mark, not a font and a colour; two
faces, a real scale, negative tracking at display size, a 58-character measure, tabular figures
in the ledger; three colour roles with the signal used once; one grid, hairlines, one rotated
element; copy in the repository's own words; one motion law, then stillness, and none under
reduced motion; the hierarchy survives at 390 px; links have hover and focus states.

**Considered and not made:** dropping the head's centre item for a purer two-item running line;
a hairline between the value and the `ok` column. Both are preferences without a defect behind
them, and a change without a named weakness is decoration. The loop ends here.
