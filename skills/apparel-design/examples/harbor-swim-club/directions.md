# Directions — Harbor Swim Club, tee (front and back)

Written before any artwork. The subject is a fictional open-water swimming club that swims year
round from a harbour wall. The brief: a tee the members will actually wear.

## DNA — from open-water swimming's own world

| Ask | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Materials** | cold water; a harbour wall; orange tow-floats; lane rope; a life-ring; the tide table pinned to the changing-room door; neoprene; a thermos |
| **Motifs** | the ring (life-ring, tow-float, buoy); the tide's rise and fall as a table of times and heights; "swim between the flags" |
| **Palette from source** | navy of deep water; the cream of a rope; the orange of a tow-float — used only where safety uses it |
| **Tempo** | dawn, weekly, regardless of weather; the timetable is the tide, not the clock |
| **Vernacular** | "high water", "slack", "a dip", "the wall", "9 degrees" |
| **Rules of the world** | you swim when the tide says; the ring is never decoration; nothing on the water is shouted |

## Directions

1. **Tonal one-ink, the tide table on the back** (one ink + the club's real artifact). Front:
   a small ring mark on the left chest. Back: the month's high-water times set as a table —
   data as the design, because the tide table *is* the club's calendar. Cream ink on navy.
   *Swap test:* another club's tide table is a different table; a club that swims a pool has
   no tide. **Survives.**
2. **Placement break** (the ring on the sleeve only, nothing else). *Swap test:* survives;
   quiet to the point of vanishing on a member tee — better as the club's cap or beanie.
   **Runner-up, for headwear.**
3. **All-over lane rope** (sublimation stripe). *Swap test:* any swim club, any pool. **Fails.**
4. **The number** — "9°" huge on the back, the water temperature. *Swap test:* survives; the
   number is the identity. But it reads as a joke tee before it reads as a club. **Discarded.**

## Committed

**Direction 1.** Screen print, one colour — cream (Pantone 7499 C) on a navy blank — so the
garment supplies the second colour. Front: the ring with the word beneath, 3.5 in, left chest,
3.5 in below the collar seam. Back: "HIGH WATER · SEPTEMBER" and twelve dawn high-water rows
(date, time, height) in a monospace, 11 in wide, top 4.5 in below the collar, with the one
line every member says at the foot: *swim between the flags.* No underbase: cream is opaque
enough on navy in one hit with a second flash if the shop prefers.

One graft from 2: the mark is small and off-centre; the tee is quiet on the front and says
everything walking away.
